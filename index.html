03How I work

Every project starts with the question, not the tool.

Whether it's a single deck or a full reporting system, the work follows the same four steps.

  1. Step 01

    Define the question

    We agree on the decision the analysis needs to support — and what success looks like.

  2. Step 02

    Gather & clean

    I collect the right data and check it properly, so the conclusions hold up.

  3. Step 03

    Analyze

    Patterns, drivers and comparisons — focused on what's relevant to your question.

  4. Step 04

    Recommend

    Clear findings and ranked next steps, in a deck or a dashboard your team can use.

One-time deck

A focused analysis, delivered.

A presentation-ready deck that answers one question well.

  • Social listening or brand health report
  • Campaign or quarterly performance review
  • Consumer or market research summary

Best for: a single decision, a stakeholder update, or a pitch.

End-to-end build

A system that keeps working.

The full pipeline, set up once, so the insight keeps updating after I'm done.

  • Automated data collection and cleaning
  • A live Power BI dashboard or reporting system
  • Documentation and a handover for your team

Best for: ongoing tracking, or teams that need repeatable reporting.

Not sure which one you need? Tell me what you're trying to decide and I'll suggest the right fit.

04About

A marketer's instincts, with an analyst's toolkit.

I'm Michel Magdy, a marketing analyst based in Egypt. My background is in marketing, so I know the questions brand teams are really asking: Is the campaign working? What are people saying about us? Where should the budget go next?

I learned the data side — Python, SQL and Power BI — so I could answer those questions properly instead of just reporting numbers. Day to day that means social and survey analysis, automating repetitive Excel reporting, and building dashboards people actually open.

I'd rather get the analysis right than make it look impressive. Every finding should connect to a decision someone can make.
